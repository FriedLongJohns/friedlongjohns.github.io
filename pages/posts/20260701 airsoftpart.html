Airsoft Part Replacement Project

My airsoft pistol broke, so I made my own parts for it.

Over the summer, I bought a cheap little airsoft springer pistol. It was pretty cool, but it's internals also shattered apart within five shots.

I don't own a 3d printer... but my friends do! I started making replacement internals for the gun.

The original parts used rubber o-rings to use gas pressure to fire the BB, but I decided to redesign the gun to launch the BB by smacking it with a metal rod.

Naturally, the design didn't work the first try.

I'd design a new version, have my friend print it, and then drive over the next day to find out the parts don't work. Wrong sizes and tolerances, bad seals, and most of all - not enough strength.

We had to redesign the parts over and over in order to withstand the forces of firing the BB; changing print orientations, thickening parts within our limited space, lengthening the firing action of the gun - anything that would let the PLA plastic hold up a little longer.

I've got a plastic bag somewhere filled with the 20 or so failed (or shattered) print versions. But the pistol works now!

The airsoft weapon originally shot at 300 fps, and now does... 100. But it's more than 0, and I had a lot of fun doing this project.
